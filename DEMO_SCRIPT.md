- **Member 2 (You):** The Business Partner Persona (*"Maria, manager at Crumb & Co. Bakery"*) & Impact Wrap-up

### [0:00 – 0:25] Hook & Problem Statement (Both / Shared Voiceover)
**Screen:** Show the RescueBite Hero section with the live impact numbers (`1,420 Meals Rescued · 3,550 kg CO₂ Saved · $11,360 Student Savings`).

**Speaker (Member 1 or 2):**
> *"Every single day around universities, local bakeries, cafes, and dining halls toss out dozens of trays of perfectly good, freshly prepared meals simply because they can't sell them before closing time. At the same time, over 30% of college students face food insecurity, skipping meals due to rising inflation.*  
>  
> *We built **RescueBite**: a fast, hyper-local platform that connects campus food spots with hungry students to rescue surplus meals at 70% off or for free, right before closing."*

---

### [0:25 – 1:05] Act 1: The Student Experience (Member 1 — Santoshi)
**Screen Actions:**
1. Switch to **Student Shelf** mode.
2. Scroll to the **Rescue Shelf** grid.
3. Hover over the urgent countdown timer (`⏰ 24m remaining`).
4. Click the **"Vegetarian"** or **"Bakery"** filter to show responsive filtering.
5. Click **"Reserve for $3.50"** on the *Campus Bakery Surprise Bag*.
6. Show the 1-click confirmation dialog and hit **"Save this box"**.
7. Zoom into the generated **Digital Pickup Pass** showing the dynamic QR code and 4-digit Rescue PIN (e.g. `RB-4821`).

**Speaker (Member 1):**
> *"I'm Alex, a student finishing late study at the library. It's 8:30 PM. I open RescueBite on my phone.*  
> *Immediately, I see Crumb & Co. Bakery has 4 pastry surprise bags left that must be collected in the next 40 minutes before closing, discounted from $12 down to just $3.50.*  
> *I tap 'Reserve'. In one click, my box is locked in. The app gives me my digital Pickup Pass with a dynamic QR code and rescue PIN `RB-4821`. I head downstairs to pick it up!"*

---

### [1:05 – 1:45] Act 2: Kitchen Hub & QR Verification (Member 2 — You)
**Screen Actions:**
1. Click the **"🏪 Kitchen Hub"** pill in the top navigation.
2. Show the **Kitchen Partner Command Hub**: Active surplus boxes, pending pickups counter (`1`), and revenue recovered.
3. Click **"Post New Surplus Meal"** modal:
   - Click one of the quick presets (e.g. *Late-Night Slice Assortment* or *Student Solidarity Free Meal Box*).
   - Point out the **"Student Solidarity Free Donation"** toggle for zero-cost meals.
   - Click **"Publish Live to Student Shelf"** and show it immediately added to inventory.
4. Click **"📱 QR Scanner"** (or switch to the Scanner terminal tab):
   - Show the simulated optical camera reticle.
   - Click the **"⚡ Quick Scan RB-4821"** button (or type `4821`).
   - Hear the audio chime and watch the **confetti burst 🎉** on screen!
   - Highlight the green verification receipt: *"Verified! Hand Over the Box — +1 Meal Rescued · +2.5 kg CO₂"*.

**Speaker (Member 2):**
> *"Now let's switch to the kitchen side. I'm Maria, the bakery manager.*  
> *With RescueBite, posting surplus takes under 30 seconds. I can pick a preset, set a discount or mark it 100% free for students in need, and publish immediately.*  
> *When Alex arrives at my counter, I open the Kitchen Redemption Terminal on our shop tablet. Alex presents his QR code—I scan it or tap his PIN. Boom! Verified. No double claims, no confusion. I hand Alex his warm meal, and our store just recouped food costs while keeping landfill waste to zero."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:10] Act 3: Real-World Impact & Closing (Both)
**Screen Actions:**
1. Switch back to the **Student View** or scroll up to the **Impact Counters**.
2. Show that the global counters have incremented in real-time (`Meals Rescued`, `CO₂ Saved`, `Student Savings`).
3. Open **"My Pass"** in the top bar to show the pass is now proudly stamped with **"✓ COLLECTED"**.

**Speaker (Member 2 & 1):**
> *"Every meal verified through RescueBite delivers immediate, measurable impact:*  
> - *Directly saves students an average of $8 per meal.*  
> - *Prevents 2.5 kg of greenhouse emissions per rescued meal.*  
> - *Helps universities hit UN Sustainable Development Goals for Zero Hunger and Responsible Consumption.*  
>  
> *RescueBite turns closing time waste into campus community nourishment. Thank you!"*
